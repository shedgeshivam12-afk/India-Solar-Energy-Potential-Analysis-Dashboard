## 📌 Project Overview

The **India Solar Energy Potential Analysis Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to explore and visualize the estimated ground-mounted solar energy potential across India using publicly available data from the National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE).

The dashboard enables users to analyze solar potential at both the state and district levels through interactive visualizations, KPIs, maps, and filters. It is intended as a data analytics and business intelligence project that demonstrates data preparation, modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard design skills in Microsoft Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze estimated solar energy potential across Indian states and districts.
- Visualize geographic distribution of solar potential.
- Explore feasible wasteland availability for ground-mounted solar installations.
- Build interactive business intelligence dashboards using Power BI.
- Demonstrate data transformation and analytical reporting techniques.
- Provide a user-friendly interface for regional solar potential exploration.

---

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This dashboard analyzes **estimated solar energy potential** based on the source dataset.

It does **not** represent:

- Actual electricity generation
- Installed solar power capacity
- Operational solar plants
- Real-time energy production
- Future project commitments

The analysis is limited to the estimated solar potential and feasible wasteland information provided in the source dataset.
